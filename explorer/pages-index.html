PRPLMESH · EASYMESH EVALUATION LAB

Explore the system. Try the room.

Interactive documentation for the container-based prplMesh lab, its real Wi-Fi software, and simulated radios.

Part of the EasyMesh optimizer goal, with the RDK EasyMesh lab, the same lab on RDK-B. The EMOSA and OpenSync labs bring existing OpenSync pods into EasyMesh. The end goal is one EasyMesh system on wmediumd, with native EasyMesh agents and OpenSync pods working together.

What it is

Native prplMesh, run as containers in one Ubuntu VM: a controller and extenders, a pool of 100 simulated clients, userspace wmediumd as the virtual RF medium, the EasyMesh optimizer, and an interactive room to play scenarios in.

These public pages do not connect to or control a live lab. The explorer is a pinned documentation snapshot; topology placements are illustrative, not live measurements.

Current state

  • Development checkpoint: fresh-VM and full-suite acceptance are pending.
  • The ordinary room catalog has passing evidence for all 24 rooms across two runs; guarded load steering passed with a native BTM.
  • Still open: geometry isolation/recovery (an extender's 6 GHz inventory disappeared) and pressure veto / weak-signal rescue (no complete live proof).
